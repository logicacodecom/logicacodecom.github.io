This policy explains what personal information logicacode collects through logicacode.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers this website and the enquiries people send us through it. It does not cover our products, such as Newsroom247, ClueXP and SESAT, which have their own terms and privacy information.

logicacode.com is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [COUNTRY / STATE OF REGISTRATION] ("logicacode", "we", "us"). We decide how the information described here is used. You can reach us about privacy at info@logicacode.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you choose and the message you write.
  • Email and phone. Whatever you share when you email or call us, including your contact details.
  • Job applications. Your CV and anything else you send when you apply for a role or introduce yourself.

Please do not send confidential business information or sensitive personal information through the contact form. If a project needs it, we will agree a secure way to share it first.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, time on page, the site that referred you, your browser and device type, and your approximate location (country or city). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers.
  • Consent record. Our consent tool, Usercentrics, stores your cookie choices so we can respect them on later visits.
  • Server and delivery logs. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, and Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces on this site, receive your IP address and basic request details when your browser loads a page. This is how any website is delivered.

We do not buy personal information from third parties, and the site has no user accounts.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Steps before a contract and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, when you contact us.
  • Consent, for analytics cookies. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interest in running and securing the site, for server logs and spam protection.
  • Steps before a contract and our legitimate interest in recruiting, for job applications.
  • Legal obligation, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.
