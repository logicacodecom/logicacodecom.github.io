# logicacode 90-day content calendar

Covers 16 November 2026 to 12 February 2027 (13 weeks). Written 11 November 2026.

**Goal:** make logicacode visible to operations leaders and senior technology leaders (CIO, CTO, chief architect) in the US and the Middle East, and turn that attention into contact form enquiries.

**Channels:** LinkedIn company page (main channel), founder LinkedIn profile, a monthly email, and six articles for the site.

## How to use this plan

- **Rhythm:** two company posts a week (Tuesday and Thursday, around 9:00 AM EST, which is late afternoon in the Gulf), one founder post a week, one article every two weeks, one email a month.
- **Every draft below is ready to edit, not ready to post.** Read each one against the rules in the next section first.
- **Articles need a home.** The site has no Insights section yet. Until it does, publish each article as a LinkedIn article from the company page, and move it to the site later.
- **Links:** LinkedIn shows fewer people a post with a link in the body. Put the link in the first comment, or use the page link when the post is about one page.
- **Images:** use `assets/img/og-share.png` as the default image, product screenshots for product posts, and real team or project photos when you have them. Do not use stock photos of robots.

## Rules for every post

1. **Write logicacode in lowercase**, everywhere.
2. **Publish only what is true and checked.** The assessment in September removed unverified numbers from the site. Do not bring them back in posts. The only figure used below is "365 UAE schools" for SESAT, taken from the live site; confirm it is still current.
3. **No client names, results or quotes** without written permission.
4. **One idea and one next step per post.**
5. **At most three hashtags.** No emojis in company posts.
6. **Reply to every comment within one working day.**

### 17. Agents versus scripts (week 9, Thu 14 Jan)
A script does the same steps every time. An AI agent is given a goal and works out the steps.

That makes agents useful where work varies: triaging requests, handling exceptions, coordinating across systems.

It also means they need limits. What may the agent do alone? When must it ask a person? What gets logged?

Decide those before you deploy. The technology is the easy part.

#AgenticAI #Automation #Governance

### 18. Data before models (week 10, Tue 19 Jan)
"Which AI model should we use?" is rarely the first question.

The first questions are about data:
- Do we have it?
- Can we reach it?
- Do we trust it?
- Who owns it?

If the answers are unclear, start with data engineering. A modest model on reliable data is more useful than an advanced model on data nobody trusts.

#Data #AI #DataEngineering
