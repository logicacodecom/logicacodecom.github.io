## Innovations dropdown

logicacode's own products and platforms.

### 1. Newsroom247
**AI-native editorial operating system** · https://newsroom247.net
_"Plan faster. Collaborate better. Publish smarter."_

One workspace for reporters, editors, designers and publishers, from story planning to publication. It replaces email, spreadsheets and old CMS tools with structured, AI-assisted teamwork, and editors stay in control.

- Eight connected modules: assignments, workflows, calendars, desk management, publishing, print planning and analytics
- Built-in SEO: headline suggestions, keywords and metadata
- Role-based permissions and real-time approval tracking
- Digital and print operations
- English and Arabic
- AI help with story development and quality checks, plus newsroom analytics

**For:** newspapers, magazines, digital news portals, government media centres, corporate communications teams, publishing groups with several brands, and editorial teams spread across locations.

### 2. ClueXP
**Urgent local service dispatch platform** · https://cluexp.com
_"One request. One accountable handoff."_

Connects customers who need urgent local help with vetted independent service companies and their technicians. ClueXP handles checks, coordination and tracking from request to completion. Home lockouts are the service supported today.

- One simple request form that asks for very little up front
- Automatic checks that the area is covered and the job is eligible
- Separate authorisation step before a technician is dispatched
- Real-time status tracking
- Providers keep their own brand and their own customer relationships

**For:** customers who need urgent help, provider companies that want extra demand, and technicians working through eligible providers. ClueXP is not a directory or a lead marketplace. It is the technology that makes each handoff accountable.

### 3. SESAT
**Advanced Library Management System**

_"Smart MARC 21 Editor: cataloguing has never been easier."_

A web-based integrated library system (ILS), ready to run in the cloud. One shared database serves many libraries, and staff and readers need nothing more than a web browser. Used in 365 schools across the UAE.

**General**
- One shared database for many libraries. New libraries can be added at any time, each with its own lending rules and fines
- Shared union catalogue across all libraries
- Sign-in through existing LDAP directories, such as Microsoft Active Directory and SharePoint
- Nothing to install for users, only a web browser. Responsive design works on any screen
- Server runs on Windows, macOS, Unix or Linux, with any database system
- Built-in search index for fast results, customisable search, and room to add new features

**Cataloguing: Smart MARC 21 Editor**
- Fully web-based, fast and easy to use
- Works for expert and new cataloguers alike
- Supports all MARC 21 tags, sub-fields and indicators, with a built-in MARC validator and help tools
- Automatic authority record linking
- Finds and imports records from outside catalogue servers (Z39.50, SRU/SRW), including the Library of Congress and OCLC
- Imports .mrc, .mrk and MARCXML files

**Circulation**
- Check-in, check-out, reservations, holds and fines

**Other modules**
- Serials management and acquisitions

**OPAC (online public access catalogue)**
- Faceted search: narrow results by format, call number, language, author, genre, era, region and more
- Suggested resources and searches, plus browsing
- Personal favourites lists, tags, comments, and sharing by email or text
- Persistent URLs, APA/MLA citations and author biographies
- Multilingual interface

**For:** schools, school networks, universities and institutional libraries.

_Registered software: ITIDA Intellectual Property Office, Egypt, deposit no. 2400, 12 Nov 2014._
