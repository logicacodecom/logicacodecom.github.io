Enterprise Architecture

A clear picture of your systems, a target state your teams agree on, and a roadmap that survives contact with delivery — built with AI and automation doing the heavy lifting.

When Teams Call Us

Architecture work usually starts with a decision that cannot be made confidently, because nobody has a complete picture of the systems involved.

Nobody can see the whole landscape

Systems have grown through projects, acquisitions, and workarounds. The documentation is out of date, and the real integrations live in people's heads.

A transformation programme has stalled

The direction was agreed, but delivery keeps hitting dependencies, unclear ownership, and decisions that were never written down.

AI pilots are not reaching production

Promising pilots stop at the point where data access, integration, security, and ownership have to be resolved for real.

The estate costs more every year

Overlapping applications, ageing platforms, and duplicated data raise cost and risk, and it is unclear what can safely be retired.

Powered by AI & Automation

Traditional architecture work is slow because most of the effort goes into gathering information rather than making decisions. We automate the gathering, so the time goes where it belongs.

  • Automated discovery — we read what your estate already tells us: inventories, cloud accounts, code repositories, and integration logs.
  • AI-assisted analysis — contracts, runbooks, and system documentation are summarised into a current-state picture your team can correct quickly.
  • AI-assisted drafting — capability maps, decision records, and roadmap options are drafted for an architect to challenge, edit, and own.
  • Automated standards checks — designs and cloud configurations are checked against the standards you agreed, not reviewed once and forgotten.
  • A living architecture — the repository is refreshed by automation, so it still reflects reality after the engagement ends.

What We Deliver

Each engagement produces something your teams can act on — not a document that is read once and filed.

Architecture assessment

A current-state view of applications, integrations, and data flows, with the risks, duplication, and constraints that matter for the decision ahead.

Target state & roadmap

Options with honest trade-offs, a recommended target state, decision records, and a sequence of moves your delivery teams can start on.

Capability map & application portfolio

What the business does, which systems support it, where they overlap, and a defensible view of what to keep, invest in, or retire.

Integration & API architecture

How systems exchange data, which patterns to standardise on, and how to reduce the point-to-point connections that make change expensive.

Cloud & data platform architecture

Platform choices, landing zones, and data foundations that make analytics and AI practical instead of a special case each time.

AI reference architecture

The pattern your AI work follows: data access, model and platform choices, security boundaries, human review, and the path from pilot to production.

Architecture governance

Standards, review practices, and design authority support that teams can actually follow — supported by automated checks rather than meetings alone.

Technology due diligence

An independent read on the state of a technology estate before an investment, acquisition, or major programme commitment.

How an Engagement Runs

The same three stages as every logicacode engagement, applied to architecture.

01

Discover

Automated discovery of the landscape, AI-assisted review of existing documentation, and interviews with the people who know how it really works.

You provide: access to inventories and the right people.
You receive: a validated current-state picture and the questions that matter.

02

Build

Target-state options, trade-offs, and decision records — with prototypes or proofs of concept where a decision needs evidence rather than opinion.

You provide: decisions at agreed checkpoints.
You receive: an agreed target state, decision records, and a sequenced roadmap.

03

Deploy

Handover into delivery, with governance, automated standards checks, and a repository that stays current as the estate changes.

You provide: the teams who will carry the work forward.
You receive: working governance and an architecture that stays accurate.
